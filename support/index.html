Quick answers

How do I restore Tally Pro on a new phone?
Install Tally, open Settings and tap Restore purchase. The store confirms the purchase and everything switches back on. On iPhone, Family Sharing is enabled, so a purchase in your family group counts.
How do I make it completely silent?
Open Settings and switch on Silent mode. It drops both the click sound and the buzz. The click never interrupts your music. It mixes over it.
I tapped twice by mistake.
Tap Undo, under the number. It takes one off. Reset takes the count back to zero for the next set.
Where are my counts stored?
On your phone, and nowhere else. There is no account and no cloud, so there is nothing to sign into and nothing to delete on a server.
Can I start from a number other than zero?
With Tally Pro, edit a counter and type any starting number, or jump to a new one mid-set.
