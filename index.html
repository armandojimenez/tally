The click

Feel everycount land.

The click is the point. Every tap answers with a sound and a buzz, tuned to land together, and the count fires the moment your thumb touches down instead of when you lift it. That is what makes it feel like a metal hand clicker.

Pick a click you can feel through gloves, or one you can barely feel at all.

In the details

The small stuff,handled.

Double tap? Undo it.

Undo takes one off, so a double tap costs you nothing. Reset starts the next set from zero.

Silent when it matters.

Silent mode drops the sound and the buzz for a library or a meeting. Your music keeps playing either way.

Awake through the set.

The screen stays on through a long count instead of locking mid-set. Switch it off any time in Settings.
